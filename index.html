About MathProject

Notes, problems, and worked solutions, written up properly.

What this is

MathProject is a collection of high-quality mathematical problems, study resources, ideas, and news from the world of mathematics. It is built for anyone who wants to practice, learn, and explore mathematics in a structured way. The focus is on quality over quantity, carefully selected problems worth solving, useful resources worth studying, and interesting mathematical developments worth knowing about. Everything is organized in one place to make it easier to discover new material, develop problem-solving skills, and go deeper into mathematics.

What you'll find

  • Problems - standalone problems with full solutions.
  • Study - longer notes on a single topic, built up over time.
  • Archive - everything, organized by subject.
  • News - updates on what's being added or reworked.

Why it looks like this

Mathematics deserves to be presented clearly. MathProject uses a clean, readable layout designed around mathematical content, so problems, solutions, proofs, and study material are easy to read and follow. The aim is not to make the site feel like a textbook, but to create a simple place where good mathematics takes center stage—with clear writing, properly typeset equations, and enough structure to make ideas easy to explore.
